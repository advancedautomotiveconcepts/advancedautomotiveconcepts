## 2025-08-10 - Mobile Menu Accessibility Enhancement
**Learning:** Custom mobile hamburger menus often lack complete accessibility cues beyond simple display toggles. Providing screen-reader users with descriptive and dynamic `aria-label` updates on the toggle button ("Open menu" vs "Close menu"), supporting standard key-dismissal behavior (pressing Escape key), and restoring focus to the triggering element are essential for fully inclusive mobile interaction. Additionally, closing the menu when clicking outside prevents overlapping layout interactions on pointer-based viewports.
**Action:** Always implement dynamic `aria-label` updates, Escape key listener (with focus restoration), and outside pointer click handlers on custom overlays and toggle menus to meet standard accessibility patterns.
